About PlacementPredict

PlacementPredict is a machine-learning based project developed to analyze student data and study the factors associated with placement outcomes.

The system combines exploratory data analysis, preprocessing, feature engineering, regression, classification, clustering, dimensionality reduction and anomaly detection.
