Project Overview

PlacementPredict EDA is a machine-learning project designed to analyze student academic, technical and personal attributes and understand their relationship with placement outcomes.

The project includes data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, regression, classification, clustering, dimensionality reduction and anomaly detection.
